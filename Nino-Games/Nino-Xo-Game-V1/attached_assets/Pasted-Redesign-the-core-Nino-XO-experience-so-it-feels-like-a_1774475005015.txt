Problems to fix:
1. too much dashboard / utility-app feeling
2. too many card-like app sections
3. playing screen animations feel too heavy / too decorative
4. board is not dominant enough
5. character tokens feel too small
6. turn feedback is too weak

9. too much empty space in key screens

ANIMATION / MOTION RULES
==================================================

This is critical.

The current play-screen animation feels too much.
Fix it.

Keep only game-useful motion:
- quick token pop-in
- subtle tile press feedback
- active turn pulse
- fast win-line reveal
- short character reaction
- quick result transition

Reduce or remove:
- slow floating motion on match screen
- long decorative animations
- heavy bounce on everything
- delayed interaction feedback
- anything that slows the feeling of play

Target:
- snappy
- immediate
- satisfying
- premium
- not noisy

Game motion must support gameplay, not distract from it.
